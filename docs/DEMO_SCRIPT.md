# 60-second demo script

Two phones side by side, both visible. Role-played, synthetic dialogue only (fictional names, prices).
Phone A: **Asha** (customer) speaks **Hinglish** (`en-IN`). Phone B: **Ravi** (shopkeeper) speaks **Marathi** (`mr-IN`). A little background chatter (TV or market sound) is fine — that is the *Noise Canceller* point.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over / dialogue |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | Title card → Saar home on both phones | "Notes from a phone call — with everyone's consent — in Hinglish and Marathi." |
| 8–18 s | A starts, shares the link; B joins. Both read the notice and tap **I agree**; A taps **Start** | "Nothing is transcribed until both people agree. Either can stop it any time." |
| 18–40 s | Captions appear on both phones, labelled | **Asha:** "Namaste dada, mujhe teen kilo chawal aur do packet doodh chahiye." · **Ravi:** "हो, तीन किलो तांदूळ आणि दोन पॅकेट दूध. एकूण दोनशे दहा रुपये." · **Asha:** "Aur ek dozen kele bhi. Shaam ko chhe baje bhej dena, mera naam Kavita hai." · **Ravi:** "ठीक आहे, संध्याकाळी सहा वाजता पाठवतो." · Tap **Quick note** |
| 40–52 s | **End call** → note opens: summary, action item "deliver by 18:00", amounts. Tap an evidence chip → transcript line highlights. Point at one ⚠ flag | "Every point cites the transcript. Anything we can't verify is flagged — not hidden." |
| 52–60 s | Eval chart (WER vs noise) → logo + hashtags | "Built on Gnani Prisma speech-to-text. #GnaniAI #GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge" |

Checklist: warm the server 5 min before; both phones on earphones; screen-record both; keep the pre-recorded fallback video ready.
